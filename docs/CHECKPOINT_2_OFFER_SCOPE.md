# Checkpoint 2 — AgentReady Local Offer Scope

Status: operational scope for campaign use, 2026-09-10

This document is the campaign source of truth for the paid offer. The locked product facts remain in `docs/OFFICIAL_PRODUCT_SOURCE.md`; this document makes the delivery boundary explicit.

## The offer

**AgentReady Local Verified Audit — $297 for one location**

The audit compares owner-approved practice facts and the practice's public web representation with observable answers from supported AI answer engines. It identifies verified inconsistencies, omissions, and machine-readability gaps, then provides a prioritized remediation roadmap.

The multi-location price is **$750**. It is a separate scope and is not included in the single-location price.

## Who it is for

The buyer is a med spa owner, aesthetic-practice owner, or practice manager responsible for one location and its public website. The buyer should provide the canonical website and enough practice information to distinguish verified facts from facts that still need confirmation.

## Included for one location

- A 100-point rubric covering identity, services and pricing, trust, machine-readable content, actions, crawl policy, security, and freshness.
- Public-page and markup inspection within the documented audit scope.
- Attempted live query tests across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overview.
- Exact queries, UTC observation times, source URLs, screenshots or extracted responses where available, confidence, and verification status.
- A prioritized five-step remediation roadmap.
- A report and evidence ZIP with a manifest, artifact hashes, and clearly labeled pending or blocked tests.

## Delivery rule

The delivery target is within 24 hours after payment and receipt of the required website/location inputs. This is a target, not a ranking or outcome guarantee. If an engine or validator is inaccessible, the audit records the attempt and marks that test pending; it never invents a response, screenshot, score, or cause.

## Required inputs

- Canonical website URL.
- Practice name, city, and location count.
- Owner-approved corrections for address, services, pricing model, providers, and booking URL when the buyer wants those facts evaluated as authoritative.
- A working email for delivery and clarification.

No shared passwords are required for the audit. We do not submit a real appointment or transmit patient/customer data.

## Not included

- Schema or website implementation. That is the separate Foundation Install: $1,500 single location or $3,500 professional multi-location.
- Ongoing retesting or monitoring. That is $249/month single location or $499/month multi-location/enterprise.
- Guaranteed rankings, citations, recommendations, traffic, bookings, revenue, or correction of a third-party AI system.
- Direct publishing into ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, or another answer engine.
- A full-site crawl, legal/medical compliance opinion, or factual endorsement of the practice by AgentReady.
- A completed result for an engine that could not be observed. Pending is not failure.

## Acceptance criteria

An audit is ready to deliver when every stated finding has a source, UTC timestamp, confidence, verification status, and evidence reference; the coverage table identifies submitted, captured, pending, and blocked tests; the report and ZIP agree; and the checksum verifies. A score is withheld or explicitly marked partial when the evidence does not support a complete score.

## Campaign language

Use: “We show what the engines returned, what the public site exposes, and what remains unverified.”

Do not use: “AI will rank you,” “schema guarantees visibility,” “we fix ChatGPT,” “most clinics fail,” or any unsupported percentage, revenue, or outcome claim.

## Conversion path

Free 6-point Surface Scan → permission to send the relevant evidence → $297 Verified Audit → optional $1,500 Foundation Install → optional $249/month Monitoring.

The first campaign CTA is **Run the free surface scan**. The audit is offered after the prospect has seen a relevant observation or requested the evidence.
