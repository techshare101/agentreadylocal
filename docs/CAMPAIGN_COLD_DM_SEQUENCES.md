* **Status:** Approved Campaign Asset (Checkpoint 2 Compliant)
* **Audience:** Med Spa Owners, Medical Directors, Practice Managers
* **Core Conversion Funnel:** Observation → Permission → Free 6-Point Scan → $297 Verified Audit

---

## Strategy & Operating Rules

1. **Never pitch an SEO retainer:** Med spa owners are fatigued by agency cold pitches promising "#1 rankings". We offer flat-fee technical verification and evidence documentation.
2. **Lead with verifiable proof:** Begin by referencing a real, observable discrepancy on a live engine.
3. **Low-friction CTA:** The first call-to-action is always **permission to send a screenshot** or **the free 6-point surface scan**. The $297 Verified Audit is introduced only after interest or after they review the observation.
4. **Strict compliance:** Never guarantee rankings, traffic, or AI model behavior. Inaccessible engines are marked pending, never guessed.
