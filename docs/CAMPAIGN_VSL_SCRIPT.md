### [0:45 – 1:45] Act 2: The Root Cause (Human Web vs. Machine Web)

**[Visual:]**  
*Switch screen share to show a typical aesthetic practice website, then right-click → View Page Source, scrolling to reveal thousands of lines of styled HTML, CSS, and image tags.*

**[Speaker Voiceover:]**  
"Most medical spa websites are built beautifully for humans. You have gorgeous photography, elegant fonts, and custom treatment menus. 

Humans love that. But AI answer engines don't look at your site like a human does.

AI crawlers read structured, machine-readable data. They look for explicit signals:
- **MedicalBusiness and Physician schema** to verify who your licensed injectors and medical directors are.
- **Service and PriceSpecification markup** to verify what treatments you perform and what they cost.
- **ReserveAction schema** so AI agents can cite an authoritative, crawlable booking URL.
- And clear **crawl directives like `llms.txt`** that tell AI models where to find your verified facts.

When those machine-readable assets are missing, AI models are forced to guess from third-party directories, forum chatter, or competitor aggregators. 

That is how discrepancies, outdated prices, and omitted services end up in live AI responses."
