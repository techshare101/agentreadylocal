# PRD: AgentReady Local — Funnel + Verified Audit Report (Phase 1: Cash)

## Company & Organization
- **Company**: MetalMindTech LLC
- **Market Reach**: High-Growth Aesthetic Markets & Med Spas (Nationwide / Universal)
- **Operating Principle**: *"Humans buy verified outcomes today; agents buy trusted resources tomorrow."*

## Overview
Phase-1 cash product for MetalMindTech LLC: a lead-gen funnel (free surface scan $\rightarrow$ $297 Verified Audit CTA) and the 100-point Verified Audit sample report (the primary sales asset). Target buyer: single/multi-location med spa owners in high-growth metro markets (Miami, Twin Cities, Dallas, Scottsdale, Los Angeles, etc.) spending on ads or SEO.

## Market & Position
- **Agency Gap**: Competitors charge $5,000–$10,000/mo or $5,999 one-time. The gap: a verifiable, flat-fee build + affordable monitor for med spas.
- **Rule**: Don't promise rankings — promise accurate, machine-readable business facts, and prove it live. The demo is the close: ask ChatGPT *"best med spa in [their city]"* live in front of the owner.

## Product Ladder
1. **Free Surface Scan**: Instant 6-point check on domain $\rightarrow$ score $\rightarrow$ 3 critical gaps $\rightarrow$ $297 paid audit CTA.
2. **$297 Verified Audit ($750 multi-location)**: 100-point scored report, live AI-engine test screenshots, timestamped evidence bundle.
3. **$1,500 Starter / $3,500 Professional Install**: Schema graph, service catalog, pricing model, FAQ/policy normalization, `llms.txt`, crawl policy, before/after test suite.
4. **$249–$499/mo Monitor**: Monthly citation re-tests, broken-schema alerts, freshness checks.
5. **(Roadmap) Agent Action Gateway**: Booking/lead endpoints, priced per completed lead.

## Non-Negotiables & Security Rules
- **No Fabricated Data**: No fake scores, rankings, testimonials, or market claims.
- **Evidence Provenance**: Every externally stated fact carries `source`, `observed_at`, `confidence`, and `verification_status`.
- **Security Rule**: Classify every exposed resource (public facts = free; booking = controlled; customer data = private).
- **Shared Evidence Flywheel**: Collect once $\rightarrow$ verify once $\rightarrow$ reuse with rights and provenance across AgentReady MedSpa, SpaSignal Intelligence, and Sovereign Operator.

## Success Metrics
- Audit $\rightarrow$ call $\ge 20\%$
- Call $\rightarrow$ install $\ge 25\%$
- Install margin $\ge 60\%$
- Monitor retention $\ge 80\%$ at 6 months
